# Telegram Violation Reporting Tool

A Telegram-focused project dedicated to account safety, violation reporting awareness, incident documentation, and responsible reporting of abusive or suspicious activity.

## Developer: https://flizoray.github.io

## Overview

Telegram Violation Reporting provides information and assistance for documenting and reporting activities that may violate Telegram's platform policies.

The project focuses on helping users understand reporting procedures involving suspicious accounts, impersonation, spam, scams, harassment, and other forms of platform abuse.

## Key Features

- Telegram violation reporting guidance.
- Account impersonation awareness.
- Spam and scam reporting assistance.
- Harassment and abusive-content documentation.
- Suspicious account identification.
- Incident and evidence documentation.
- Telegram account security awareness.
- Official reporting resources.

## Common Telegram Violations

Examples of activities users may need to report include:

- Impersonation and fraudulent identities.
- Phishing attempts and malicious links.
- Unsolicited spam messages.
- Financial scams and fraudulent promotions.
- Harassment, threats, and abusive behaviour.
- Distribution of prohibited content.

## How Telegram Violation Reporting Works

Users can identify suspicious activity, preserve relevant evidence, and submit reports through Telegram's available reporting channels.

Reports should be accurate, supported by relevant information, and submitted for genuine policy violations.

Third-party reporting tools cannot guarantee account restrictions or bans. Enforcement decisions remain under Telegram's control.
